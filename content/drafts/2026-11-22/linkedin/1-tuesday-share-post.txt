SAP has acquired TechWolf — context graph becomes core

SAP says TechWolf’s proprietary context graph will be the grounding layer for Joule queries across SuccessFactors. Treat that as a strategic product direction, not an immediate technical contract change. The implication is straightforward: if TechWolf becomes the canonical skills graph, integration, ownership and compliance responsibilities shift toward an external vendor-managed surface.

My advice: pilot first, insist on schema-stability SLAs, and protect authority for deterministic HR outcomes. Don’t cut over critical decisions until you have contractual guardrails and measurable pilot evidence.
