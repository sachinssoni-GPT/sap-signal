A 30-hour global cutover, no Plan B.

I walk a cutover from the control room to the middleware queue and explain what an architect must check first when the entire landscape must convert in a single short window. Interfaces, conversion mechanics, custom code and clear operational ownership are the places where a tight, global brownfield cutover will break — and where you can prevent the worst outcomes.

If you run SAP landscapes, the difference between a survivable 30‑hour window and a costly outage is what you verify before the clock starts.
