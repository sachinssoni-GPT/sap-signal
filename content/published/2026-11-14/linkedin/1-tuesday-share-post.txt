Four unauthenticated, maximum-severity flaws.

This November SAP published four critical vulnerabilities affecting ICM, Web Dispatcher and Gateway — two of them carry a CVSS 10.0 and allow unauthenticated impact. Treat any public-facing dispatcher, ICM or Gateway endpoint as compromised until you’ve proven otherwise.

Run an external exposure sweep and a privileged-session audit first; patch public dispatchers and exposed Gateway instances next. Two other items in the issue: SAP publishes product-level cloud emissions data, and SAP Business Suite 7 maintenance deadlines.
