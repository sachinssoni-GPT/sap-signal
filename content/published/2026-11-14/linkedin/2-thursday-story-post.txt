You get used to routine programme calls: release windows, test schedules, the usual snag list. Then security drops a bulletin and the meeting changes tone—operations and integration start naming Web Dispatcher, ICM and Gateway.

After twenty years of running SAP landscapes, the thing that keeps showing up is this: unauthenticated, maximum-severity flaws collapse planning into triage. They don’t let you debate trade-offs. They force a binary question—are we exposed or not?

So here’s a practical rule I use: stop at the edge first. If an admin console or dispatcher is reachable from untrusted networks, isolate it before you reach for the patch notes. Run a quick external sweep and kill any unexpected privileged sessions. Those two actions buy you time to test and deploy vendor fixes without assuming you’re starting from “clean”.
